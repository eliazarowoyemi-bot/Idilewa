❔ How to play — simple instructions
  1. Wait for your name and colour to light up.
  2. Roll the animated dice.
  3. Pop exactly the number of balloons shown.
  4. Watch your token move, or choose a highlighted token if more than one can move.
  5. Pass the device to the next player.
  6. Catch-up challenges let trailing players buzz in to earn bonus steps.
  7. Bring all four tokens home to win!
